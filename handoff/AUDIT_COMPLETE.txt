1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

WHAT THIS WORK IS

This work is an offline UPLIFT STUDY, not a deployed product change. It asks one
question: on the same priced MLB universe the live product sees, can a fitted
statistical model select a board of bets that beats the vig, measured under the
product's own pass/fail gate, without ever reading the data it is judged on?

The answer it returns is: on 6 of 11 markets, yes. On 5, no.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT IN PRODUCTION -- READ THIS FIRST

The live SharpAI product generates its picks from
`supabase/functions/_shared/scoring_mlb_v2.ts` plus the `algorithm_weights`
table, written into `recommendations_cache` by scheduled edge functions. That is
a hand-weighted factor scorer. Verified by direct inspection of the import graph.

THE VALIDATED MODELS (`harness/uplift/*.py`) ARE A SEPARATE CODEBASE. They import
nothing from `scoring_mlb_v2.ts` and nothing imports them. Grep across the whole
repository confirms this:

    harness/run_backtest.ts        imports scoring_mlb_v2.ts   (production scorer)
    harness/lib/*.ts               imports scoring_mlb_v2.ts   (production scorer)
    harness/uplift/*.py            imports it NOWHERE

So:
  * NO MODEL FROM THIS WORK IS SERVING TRAFFIC TODAY.
  * "Production parity PASS" does NOT mean "these models are live". It means every row
    this board selects would pass the shipped eligibility predicates if it
    were shown -- a necessary condition for deployment, not evidence of it.
  * Deploying these models would be a new engineering project (section 30.4).

WHAT THIS AUDIT FOUND THAT PRIOR DOCUMENTS DID NOT

  * A documentation error in the validation report: it quotes 535,961 graded model
    rows and 5,397 games. Those are the T-1h dataset's numbers. The dataset that
    actually produced the shipped table is T-6h, with 529,695 graded rows and
    5,421 games. Section 7.3.
  * `robustness_v1.csv` is a byte-identical copy of `robustness_v1.csv`,
    made so `final_matrix.py --tag=_v1` could find it. Benign, undocumented.
    Section 27.4.
  * `reports/calibration.csv` was computed on the PRE-expansion cache and is a
    An earlier stage diagnostic, not the final configuration one. Its conclusions still hold but its
    row counts do not match the final dataset. Section 18.3.
  * The working tree at audit time carried an uncommitted later draft of
    policy.py. The handoff ships the committed delivered file. Section 38.2.

CONFIDENCE IN THE RESULT

The result stage reproduced BIT-IDENTICALLY when re-run during this audit
(sha256 match on all 37 columns of the final table). The gate is a faithful port
of the shipped TypeScript, verified by replay. No confirmed leakage was found by
the implemented checks. The main reasons to hold the result loosely are named in
sections 43 and 44 and are about generalization, not about arithmetic.

3.3 DUPLICATE AND NEAR-DUPLICATE IMPLEMENTATIONS (found, not removed)

  * run_final.py / run_final_v2.py / run_final_model.py are three runners that
    share ~90% of their code. r4 is the shipped one. This is real duplication
    and a maintenance risk; it is also why each configuration's result can still be
    reproduced exactly, which is why nothing was collapsed.
  * policy.board and boardfast.Fast.board_mask implement the same filter twice.
    This is deliberate and guarded by selfcheck().
  * mlbgate.py duplicates harness/lib/metrics.ts in Python. Deliberate, and
    guarded by verify_gate.py.
  * Six cache directories (cache, cache_v1, cache_v2, cache_h, cache_repro,
    cache_expanded, cache_expanded_6h, cache_fam) total ~2.6 GB and hold the
    member scores for earlier configurations. Only cache_fam matters to the final configuration.

3.4 DEAD CODE

No file in harness/uplift was found to be unreachable from every entry point.
The root-level `*.py` scripts in betgenius/ (add_factors.py, tomorrow_*.py,
wire_*.py, etc.) are one-off algorithm-tuning scripts from the live product's
history; CLAUDE.md states they are "not part of the build". They are shipped in
project/ for completeness and are NOT part of the final configuration.

4. ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

4.1 THE TWO SYSTEMS

    SYSTEM A -- the live product (deployed)
        The Odds API + ESPN + Baseball Savant
              |
        Supabase edge functions (fetch-odds-mlb, process-games-mlb, ...)
              |
        scoring_mlb_v2.ts  x  algorithm_weights   <-- hand-weighted factor scorer
              |
        recommendations_cache  ->  React frontend  ->  subscriber
              |
        resolve-picks  ->  settled results

    SYSTEM B -- Phase 1 validation (offline, read-only)
        B1. harness/run_backtest.ts
            Re-scores historical candidates through the REAL production scorer
            with the SHIPPED weights, no refitting. Answers "does the product
            as shipped beat the vig?"
        B2. harness/uplift/*.py                        <-- THE VALIDATION WORK LIVES HERE
            Fits its own models on the same priced universe. Answers "could a
            fitted model do better?"

    B2 shares the priced universe, the gate and the eligibility predicates with
    A. It shares no scoring code with A.

4.2 THE VALIDATION PIPELINE, STAGE BY STAGE

    RAW DATA
      Supabase warehouse export (dump_odds.ts, dump_box.ts) -> odds_*.csv,
      boxscores.csv. Plus an Odds API expansion pull (ingest_expand.py) ->
      apix6h_*.jsonl -> build_expanded_data.py -> odds_*_api.csv.
                                |
    INGESTION / NORMALIZATION   frames.read_market
      Per (event, market, line): best and median two-sided price at the entry
      snapshot; de-vigged fair probability; book count.
                                |
    DEDUPLICATION
      One row per (event, market, line). An alternate ladder stays as multiple
      rows here and is collapsed to one bet later, at board time, by
      `one_per=unit_key(market)`.
                                |
    MARKET CANDIDATES           frames.load_candidates
      Requires: a matched game_pk; a resolvable player id (ambiguous names are
      dropped, never guessed); BOTH sides quoted.
                                |
    OUTCOME GRADING             frames.load_candidates
      Against the box-score stat for that (player, game) or the team score for a
      game market. Pushes -> NaN -> excluded from grading.
                                |
    POINT-IN-TIME FEATURES      features.build
      Every roll-up reads strictly EARLIER local calendar dates.
                                |
    MODEL TRAINING + PREDICTION policy.walkforward
      Expanding-window walk-forward, 40 blocks, 25% warm-up, fold boundaries on
      the clock. Produces one out-of-sample probability per row per member.
                                |
    CALIBRATION
      Not a separate stage in the shipped config. The estimator IS the
      calibrator: it is fit on outcomes, so it emits a probability directly.
      Optional post-hoc isotonic exists as the `iso` member and was not adopted.
                                |
    EDGE                        policy.board
      ev_over = p*dec_over - (1-p);  ev_under = (1-p)*dec_under - p
                                |
    CONFIDENCE                  policy.board
      round(100 * p_of_the_side_being_bet)
                                |
    POLICY FILTER               policy.board + mlbgate
      confidence >= 60; EV > 0.02 (prop markets only); side policy; D-164
      heavy-juice under veto; ladder collapse on max EV.
                                |
    BOARD                       one row per (game, player) or per game
                                |
    EVALUATION                  mlbgate.grade + mlbgate.verdict
      Two windows: full OOS and the verdict window. Same gate.

5. DATA PIPELINE
================================================================================

5.1 WAREHOUSE EXPORT (TypeScript, read-only)

`harness/uplift/dump_odds.ts` selects, per event and market, the earliest
snapshot strictly before commence_time, and from that snapshot the BEST
two-sided price at each line plus the MEDIAN across books. `dump_box.ts` exports
`cache_mlb_boxscore_player_stats`. `dump_events.ts` exports the event list.
All three are SELECT-only; the harness role is `harness_readonly` with a
connection limit of 10 (CLAUDE.md), and the project rule is never to run two
harness processes at once.

5.2 ODDS API EXPANSION (Python)

`probe_api_coverage.py` establishes what exists before anything is spent.
`ingest_expand.py --plan` prices the pull; `--run --lead=6 --suffix=6h` executes
it, requesting the snapshot 6 hours before first pitch.
`build_expanded_data.py` converts the JSONL to the same CSV schema the warehouse
export produces, writing to `data_expanded_6h/`. `data/` is never written to,
which is why the earlier baseline result still reproduces from the original inputs.

5.3 ASSEMBLY

`UPLIFT_DATA_DIR` selects the dataset. `UPLIFT_CACHE_DIR` selects the member
cache. Both default to the original paths, so an unset environment reproduces
the earlier baseline result and a set one reproduces the final result. This is the mechanism that
lets several configurations coexist without any of them being overwritten.

5.4 THE EVENT_ID <-> GAME_PK BRIDGE

`cache_mlb_historical_events` runs only to 2026-06, and for 2026 games the
warehouse never priced it stores a synthetic `mlb_<game_pk>` id that the provider
rejects with HTTP 422. Past that point the bridge is rebuilt from team names plus
the UTC game date and was VALIDATED BEFORE USE against the rows where the
warehouse does store the true game_pk:

    events with ground-truth game_pk        5,726
    resolved by the rebuilt bridge          5,384  (94.0%)
    of those, correct                       5,337  (99.13%)
    restricted to 2026-06 (the adjacent tail) 248/248 correct, 0 wrong

An earlier version of the rule derived a US LOCAL date by shifting back eight
hours; measured against the same ground truth it resolved 550 events to the
WRONG GAME. The measurement caught it. The shipped rule uses the UTC date,
because that is what `game_date` empirically equals in 5,648 of 5,726 cases.

AUDIT NOTE: 0.87% of bridged events are known-wrong and are carried. On a
529,695-row graded universe that is a small, quantified contamination, and it is
the only one the project has measured to this precision. It is not zero.

================================================================================
9. ODDS INGESTION
================================================================================

9.1 SNAPSHOT SELECTION
The entry price is the earliest snapshot STRICTLY BEFORE commence_time. That is
what `dump_odds.ts` selects from the warehouse and what `ingest_expand.py`
requests from the API at a configured lead.

9.2 BOOKMAKER SELECTION AND PRICE NORMALIZATION
`price="best"` takes the best number quoted AT THAT LINE at the entry snapshot.
This is the shipped convention, not a choice made by the research:
`supabase/functions/_shared/best_price.ts selectBestSameLineBook` is what the
production write path and `harness/lib/game_candidates.ts` both use. A backtest
priced any other way would not be measuring the same board.

`price="median"` bets the median book instead and is reported alongside every
verdict as the no-line-shopping sensitivity (robustness variant 5). It is worth
roughly two thirds of the verdict units -- see section 27.

Implied probabilities are stored per side (`imp_over_best`, `imp_under_best`,
`imp_over_med`, `imp_under_med`) and converted back to American odds for
grading. The de-vigged fair probability is `p_fair_over = ps / (ps + po)` --
proportional normalization, the same method as `harness/lib/oddsmath.ts noVigProb`.

9.3 LINE HANDLING
One candidate row per (event, market, line). Alternate lines are kept as
separate candidates through feature building and collapsed to ONE BET at board
time by `one_per=policy.unit_key(market)`, keeping the row with the highest EV
(`collapse="maxEv"`). The alternative collapse -- `mostBooks`, the most liquid
line -- exists and was swept; maxEv won on the SELECT halves.

For spreads the `line` column is the HOME number. "plus" means whichever side
RECEIVES the number; on an MLB runline the home side is the plus side when the
home line is +1.5 and the away side is when it is -1.5.

9.4 MISSING, STALE AND DUPLICATE ODDS
  * A candidate with only one side quoted is dropped (`n_twoway > 0` and
    `p_fair_over` not null are both required).
  * A candidate with no `imp_over` or `imp_under` at the chosen price convention
    is dropped.
  * Staleness is not separately modelled -- the entry snapshot is by
    construction the one closest to the configured lead, and the warehouse holds
    roughly 2 snapshots per event, which is too sparse to reconstruct line
    movement. Capturing a denser series is production recommendation 5.

9.5 TIMEZONE HANDLING -- THE DEFECT THAT PRODUCED A FAKE EDGE ONCE
`game_date` is read as TEXT and truncated to 10 characters, never parsed as a
timestamp. Read as a timestamp it arrives shifted by the client timezone, which
moves a late start across midnight and lets an as-of join match a game to its
own box score. `frames.py` states this at the top of the file as rule 1 of
three, each of which has produced a fake edge at least once.

`commence_time` is UTC throughout. The local calendar date used for as-of joins
comes from the BOX SCORE, never from commence_time -- a 10pm start is already
tomorrow in UTC.


================================================================================
10. TIMESTAMP / ENTRY-TIME METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

This section covers the single most consequential parameter in this work.

10.1 THE MEASUREMENT
`probe_entry_lead.ts` measures the warehouse's own entry snapshot -- min(snapshot
_timestamp) strictly before commence_time, which is exactly the row dump_odds.ts
selects:

    2024-03 -> 2025-10      6.01 hours before first pitch
    2026-04 -> 2026-05      1.01 hours before first pitch

The WAREHOUSE ITSELF contains a convention break at the 2026 boundary. It was
not introduced by any ingest; the production snapshot scheduler changed.

This audit independently corroborated the break by a second route: average lead
across ALL snapshots per month is 2.2-2.4 h through 2025-10 and 1.0 h in
2026-04/05 (read-only SQL, section 6).

10.2 WHY IT MATTERS
A one-hour line has absorbed five more hours of market information than a
six-hour line. Edge measured against it is systematically smaller. The first
expansion pull (`ingest_odds_api.ts`) used T-1h, which DEEPENED a break the
project already contained.

10.3 WHAT IT WAS WORTH -- MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED

    entry convention               verdict PASS   both-gates PASS
    T-1h  (first pull)                 2/11             2
    T-6h  (matches the warehouse)      5/11             5

Three markets turned on one parameter -- more than the 30% extra data was worth.
All 2,111 events were re-pulled at lead=6h and the served lead verified at a
6.01 h median.

10.4 RESIDUAL, DISCLOSED
2026 WAREHOUSE rows remain at their native ~1.01 h while 2026 API rows are at
6 h. The project carries this rather than overwriting authoritative warehouse
values. It is a known inconsistency inside the shipped dataset, and it is one
plausible contributor to the 2026 degradation in section 44.

10.5 SEMANTICS, STATED
    commence_time        event start, UTC, from the provider
    snapshot_timestamp   when the price was OBSERVED -- a real observation time,
                         not a proxy, not a settlement time
    entry price          the price at the earliest snapshot strictly before
                         commence_time, at the configured lead
    game_date            local calendar date, read as text (see 9.5)
    feature as-of cut    strictly an EARLIER local calendar date than the game


================================================================================
11. CANDIDATE GENERATION
================================================================================

`frames.load_candidates(market, box, price)` produces one row per
(event, market, line), and applies these filters in order:

  1. The event must map to a `game_pk` that exists in the box score. The local
     game date is taken from the box score.
  2. BOTH sides must have been quoted (`n_twoway > 0`, `p_fair_over` not null).
  3. Both implied probabilities must exist at the chosen price convention.
  4. For prop markets: the player name must resolve to exactly one player_id
     globally. Ambiguous names are dropped.
  5. For game markets: both team names must resolve to team ids, and both team
     scores must exist.
  6. The settled stat must exist (section 12).

The funnel is instrumented. `frames.LAST_FUNNEL` records, per market, the priced
count, the DID_NOT_PLAY count, the DATA_MISSING count and the withOutcome count;
`inventory_expanded.py` writes them to reports/inventory_exp6.csv (section 7.4).

A UNIT OF BETTING is a player-game for prop markets and a game for game markets
(`policy.unit_key`). That is what stops an alternate ladder counting as eight
bets on one player.


================================================================================
12. OUTCOME GRADING
================================================================================

12.1 PROP MARKETS
The settled stat is read from the box score at (player_id, game_pk):

    batter_hits        -> hits
    batter_total_bases -> total_bases
    batter_home_runs   -> home_runs
    batter_rbis        -> rbi
    batter_runs_scored -> runs_scored
    batter_strikeouts  -> batter_strikeouts
    pitcher_strikeouts -> strikeouts
    pitcher_outs       -> outs_derived

`outs_derived` is computed from `innings_pitched`, the decimal shorthand where
5.1 means five and one third: floor(ip)*3 + round(frac*3). The stored `outs`
column agrees wherever both exist but is null for most of 2025, so outs is
derived from IP everywhere instead -- consistently, not selectively.

    overHit = NaN            if actual == line   (a push)
    overHit = actual > line  otherwise

12.2 GAME MARKETS
Team runs come from the OPPOSING staff's charged runs: a team's pitchers' runs
sum to what the other side scored. That column is populated for every game in
the table where the batters' own runs_scored column is not.

    totals   overHit = (home+away) > line, NaN on an exact push
    h2h      overHit = home margin > 0     ("over" = home)
    spreads  overHit = (margin + line) > 0, NaN on an exact push
             (line is the home number)

12.3 WHAT IS NEVER READ
Nothing from the game being bet is read except the settled stat itself, and that
only to grade. This is rule 3 of the three stated at the top of frames.py.

================================================================================
14. POINT-IN-TIME / AS-OF INTEGRITY
================================================================================

14.1 THE THREE RULES (stated at the top of frames.py, each having produced a
     fake edge at least once)
  1. Dates are LOCAL CALENDAR DATES, as text.
  2. As-of joins are STRICTLY earlier: a feature for a game on date D may read
     box scores from dates < D only, never == D.
  3. Nothing is read from the game being bet except the settled stat, and that
     only to grade.

14.2 THE CONTROL THAT MAKES THE TEST MEANINGFUL
`check_asof.py` recomputes sampled feature rows the slow way and compares. A
test that passes because nothing moves is not a test, so it ALSO reports how
many sampled rows WOULD change if the game's own day were admitted:

    starter workload   200 rows recomputed, 0 mismatches; 188 of 200 would change
    bullpen            200 rows recomputed, 0 mismatches; 179 of 200 would change
    park               200 rows recomputed, 0 mismatches; 195 of 200 would change

That is the shape of a real as-of test: it passes, AND it would fail loudly if
the cut were wrong.

14.3 TRAINING-TIME INTEGRITY
`policy.walkforward` makes fold boundaries on the CLOCK, not on row index.
Picks that share a commence time straddle a row boundary, and that leak alone
has turned a FAIL into a PASS before now in this project. A game therefore lands
wholly on one side of a fold boundary.

14.4 LIMITS OF THE CHECK
  * 200 rows per family is a sample, not a proof.
  * Three feature families were sampled, not thirteen.
  * The bridge error rate of 0.87% (section 5.4) is a data-integrity issue
    rather than a temporal one, and it is not covered by these checks.

================================================================================
16. MODEL TRAINING
================================================================================

16.1 TARGET
Binary. `overHit` = 1 if the settled stat cleared the posted line (or the home
side won / covered), 0 otherwise, NaN on a push. NaN rows are excluded before
any fit. The model always predicts P(OVER) -- or P(HOME) on h2h and spreads --
and the board decides which side to bet afterwards, from that one probability.

16.2 TRAINING POPULATION
Every graded candidate for that market whose commence time is strictly before
the fold boundary. No down-sampling, no class balancing, no re-weighting.

16.3 THE WALK-FORWARD SCHEDULE
    warm-up      25% of rows, never scored -- they exist only to train fold 1
    blocks       40
    boundaries   TIMESTAMPS derived from the data, so a game never straddles one
    window       EXPANDING. Fold k trains on everything before its boundary.
    minimum      a fold is skipped if the training side holds under 200 rows
    verified     40 usable folds in every one of the 11 markets

16.4 REFIT
Every fold refits from scratch. There is no warm start and no incremental
update. Each of the 440 shipped estimators sees only data that had already
happened at its boundary.

16.5 MISSING VALUES, SCALING, CATEGORICALS
    gbm    HistGradientBoosting handles NaN natively. Columns that are constant
           or all-missing INSIDE a fold are dropped for that fold (fewer than 20
           finite values, or standard deviation below 1e-12) -- dropped rather
           than allowed to kill the whole member on one thin fold.
    rf     median-imputed using the TRAINING fold's medians only, then
           standardised using the TRAINING fold's mean and standard deviation.
    price / box / compact
           same training-fold-only impute-and-standardise.
    There are no categorical features. Every column is numeric by construction;
    `features.build` coerces with `pd.to_numeric(errors="coerce")` and maps
    infinities to NaN.

16.6 FEATURE SELECTION
None, in the adopted configuration. Every member is handed the market's whole
feature vector. The `compact` member (a hand-picked 13-14 column subset for thin
markets) is the only selection experiment and it was not adopted anywhere.

16.7 THE ANSWER TO "IS EVERY PREDICTION OUT OF SAMPLE?"
Yes, for the model. Every scored row was predicted by an estimator fit only on
rows whose commence time was strictly earlier than that row's fold boundary,
using features that read only strictly earlier calendar dates. Rows inside the
warm-up window are never scored at all.

The qualification is at the SELECTION layer, not the training layer: the global
filter and the per-market override were chosen using the SELECT half of the same
out-of-sample period. That is why the project reports TWO gates, and why the
verdict window exists (sections 25-26).

17. MODEL SELECTION
================================================================================

17.1 THE GLOBAL FILTER
Chosen once, over the pooled SELECT halves of all 11 markets, by
`run_final.choose_global` sweeping roughly 4,600 configurations (spec x EV floor
x confidence x ladder collapse). Result, reproduced during this audit:

    spec = gbm    EV floor = 0.02    confidence floor = 60    collapse = maxEv

The confidence floor is PINNED at 60 and was never searched. It is the shipped
product's own floor.

17.2 THE SIDE POLICY
Chosen per market on the SELECT half only (`run_final.choose_side`). This is the
same lever the shipped `MLB_EV_SIDE_POLICY` already pulls in production on hits,
total bases, game_total and game_side. Result: `under` on four markets,
`both` on seven.

17.3 THE PER-MARKET MEMBER OVERRIDE -- THE ONE ARCHITECTURAL CHANGE IN THE FINAL CONFIGURATION

A market may replace the global ensemble with a single member only if ALL FOUR
conditions hold:

  1. The SAME member wins on SELECT-A at ALL THREE inner cuts (q = 0.5/0.6/0.7).
  2. It beats the global spec on held-out SELECT-B by more than 0.0011 -- the
     noise band measured by experiments E8/E16/E19 -- at all three cuts.
  3. Its SELECT board holds at least 500 rows (EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED, the gate's
     own floor).
  4. Otherwise the market keeps the global spec, unchanged.

Decided on LOG LOSS -- a proper scoring rule over every scored row -- not on
board ROI over a few hundred rows the model selected using its own largest
errors. The verdict window is never read.

17.4 A FAILURE WORTH RECORDING -- AND IT IS RECORDED
Run WITHOUT condition 3, the rule adopted four overrides and made the result
WORSE (both-gates 5 -> 4):

    market              override  full-OOS board n     outcome
    batter_total_bases  rf        409 -> 5,356         repaired; verdict
                                                       -15.07% -> +1.07% PASS
    pitcher_outs        price     1,957 -> 507         PASS -> FAIL
    pitcher_strikeouts  price     2,407 -> 1           NO_DATA

The mechanism is structural, not statistical. A prop board is `evPerUnit > tau`,
and EV is positive only where the model DISAGREES with the price. A member
better calibrated to the price agrees with it more and produces a smaller board;
`price` agrees exactly and produces almost none. Log loss cannot see this,
because log loss scores every row while the gate scores only the selected ones.

Condition 3 is derived from the gate's own definition -- SELECT and VERDICT are
equal halves, so a SELECT board under 500 projects to a verdict board under 500
-- and is evaluated entirely on SELECT.

AUDIT POSITION ON THIS: the project discloses, in
`docs/methodology/EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md` Addendum 3, that the verdict consequence of the unamended
rule was SEEN BEFORE the amendment was written. That is the honest disclosure of
a real researcher-degree-of-freedom. It is not laundered as "condition 3 was
specified first". An auditor should note that condition 3 is nonetheless
justified from the gate's own constant rather than tuned, and that the market it
saved (pitcher_strikeouts at 1 board row) is an obvious degenerate case. The
disclosure is adequate; the exposure is real and small.

18. PROBABILITY CALIBRATION
================================================================================

18.1 IS THERE A SEPARATE CALIBRATION STAGE? NO.
In the shipped configuration the estimator IS the calibrator. `gbm`, `rf` and
`price` are all fit on binary outcomes and emit a probability directly. No
Platt scaling, no isotonic map, no temperature.

18.2 THE CALIBRATION MEMBER THAT WAS BUILT AND NOT ADOPTED
`policy._fit_predict_iso` (the `iso` member) splits the TRAINING fold itself on
the clock: fits on the earlier 80%, fits an isotonic map on the last 20% it has
not seen, refits on the whole fold, applies the map. Nothing from the test block
is used at either stage. It is point-in-time correct BY CONSTRUCTION and it was
rejected because it was inside the noise band everywhere -- not because of any
integrity concern.

================================================================================
19. EDGE CALCULATION
================================================================================

19.1 THE ARITHMETIC (policy.board)

    dec_over  = american_to_decimal(overOdds)     net profit on a 1u win
    dec_under = american_to_decimal(underOdds)
    ev_over   = p * dec_over  - (1 - p)
    ev_under  = (1 - p) * dec_under - p
    take_over = ev_over >= ev_under
    evPerUnit = max(ev_over, ev_under)

This is expected value per unit staked at the ACTUAL posted price, vig included.
A positive evPerUnit is exactly the condition "the model's probability beats the
price the book is offering".

19.2 THE FLOOR
`tau = 0.02`, applied on prop markets only. Game markets are scored on the
`ev_pass` surface, which has no EV filter at all, because the shipped game write
path carries no evPerUnit (section 28).

19.3 THE MOST IMPORTANT MEASUREMENT IN THE FINAL CONFIGURATION (E23-MEAS)

Predicted edge against realised edge, SELECT half, pooled over 11 markets:

    predicted bucket        n     predicted   realised    gap
    0-1%               11,146       0.49%      -3.20%    3.69
    1-2%                9,594       1.49%      -1.48%    2.96
    2-3%                7,939       2.49%      -0.73%    3.21
    3-5%               12,880       3.96%      -2.00%    5.96
    5-7%                9,844       5.97%      +1.60%    4.37
    7-10%              10,759       8.40%      +0.67%    7.74
    10%+               21,144      19.61%      +1.95%   17.66

A NOMINAL EDGE IS OVERSTATED BY 3 TO 18 PERCENTAGE POINTS AT EVERY LEVEL, and
the relationship to realised edge is weak and non-monotone.

This is the winner's curse, and it is structural: selecting on `ev > tau`
selects the rows where the model's error is largest AND positive. A better
probability model does not fix a selection problem.

Correcting it directly (E23: an isotonic map from predicted EV to realised
profit, fitted chronologically) LIFTS held-out ROI on 6 of 8 markets but cuts
board size 50-75%, so total units fall (24,278 vs 34,062) and it was rejected on
its own pre-registered criterion.

AUDIT POSITION: this measurement should govern how any client reads a
"predicted edge" number from this system. The EV filter works as a THRESHOLD
that produces a profitable board on six markets. It does not work as a RANKING
signal, and a 10% predicted edge is not ten times better than a 1% one.


================================================================================
20. CONFIDENCE CALCULATION
================================================================================

    confidence = round(100 * p_of_the_side_being_bet)

That is the whole definition (policy.board). If the board takes the over,
confidence is round(100*p); if it takes the under, round(100*(1-p)).

It is therefore a CALIBRATED PROBABILITY expressed on a 0-100 scale, not a
separate score and not a rating. A confidence of 64 means the model assigns 64%
to that side.

The floor is 60, pinned. It is the shipped product's own floor
(`mlbgate.ev_pass_mask` requires `confidence >= 60`) and it was never searched,
never lowered, and is identical across all 11 markets.

CONSEQUENCE WORTH STATING: because confidence is max(p, 1-p) after the side is
chosen, it is bounded below by 50 and a 60 floor is not a demanding filter on a
market whose probabilities cluster near 0.5. On batter_rbis, where the base rate
is 0.289, nearly every row clears 60 on the under side -- which is visible in the
live board (section 32): 5,129 of 5,183 predictions clear the floor. The EV
floor, not the confidence floor, is what actually cuts that board down to 111.

21. MARKET POLICIES
================================================================================

21.1 THE SHIPPED THE FINAL CONFIGURATION POLICY, IN FULL

    global spec              gbm
    EV floor (tau)           0.02
    confidence floor         60
    ladder collapse          maxEv
    per-market overrides     spreads -> price;  batter_total_bases -> rf
    side policy              per market, chosen on SELECT (table in section 8)
    parity surface           ev_pass on h2h/spreads/totals; ev_filtered elsewhere
    heavy-juice veto         D-164, unchanged
    unit of betting          (game_pk, playerId) for props; (game_pk) for games

21.2 THE D-164 HEAVY-JUICE UNDER VETO (mlbgate.is_unbettable_juice)
Applies to the UNDER side only; over, home and away are never flagged.

    confidence >= 90 AND odds <= -350   -> veto
    confidence >= 80 AND odds <= -300   -> veto
    confidence >= 70 AND odds <= -250   -> veto
    confidence >= 60 AND odds <= -200   -> veto

This is a product rule shipped in the live app. The pipeline applies it verbatim and
did NOT loosen it, even where loosening it would have produced a pass:
`batter_home_runs` has 59,338 graded candidates and 29 board rows, and the veto
plus the confidence floor is why. Loosening a product rule to pass a gate was
refused. Section 43.1.

21.3 THE OVER-BREAKEVEN RULE
`mlbgate.passes_over_breakeven` requires confidence/100 >= implied_prob(odds)
for a pick whose side is literally called "over". `policy.board` does NOT apply
it; `parity_audit.py` measures how many rows it would reject. The answer is 0
(section 28).

21.4 DUPLICATE HANDLING
An alternate ladder is collapsed to one bet per unit key, keeping the row with
the highest evPerUnit. `mostBooks` (the most liquid line) was the alternative and
lost on the SELECT halves.


================================================================================
22. BETTING DECISION LOGIC
================================================================================

Reading policy.board end to end, a candidate becomes a board row if and only if:

  1. The walk-forward probability for that row is finite (it is outside the
     warm-up window and its fold had enough data).
  2. confidence >= 60.
  3. On a prop market: evPerUnit > 0.02. On a game market: no EV condition.
  4. The chosen side matches the market's side policy, if it has one.
  5. It is not vetoed by the D-164 heavy-juice rule.
  6. It is the highest-EV row for its (game, player) -- or its game.

The side is NOT chosen by the side policy. The side is chosen by EV, and the
side policy then DISCARDS rows whose EV-chosen side is the wrong one. This
matters: an `under`-policy market does not bet the under on every row; it bets
the under on the rows where the under had the better EV, and shows nothing on
the rest.

23. OFFICIAL GATE LOGIC

23.1 THE RULE
    PASS if   graded n >= 500  AND  ROI > 0
    PASS if   graded n <  500  AND  95% ROI CI lower bound > 0
    FAIL otherwise
    NO_DATA if graded n == 0

23.2 VERIFICATION AGAINST THE SHIPPED TYPESCRIPT
`harness/lib/metrics.ts` lines 316-390:

    const EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED = 500;
    const pass = tier.graded >= EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED
        ? tier.roiPct > 0
        : tier.roiCiLoPct > 0;

`harness/uplift/mlbgate.py verdict()`:

    if m["graded"] >= EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED:   return "PASS" if m["roiPct"] > 0 ...
    if m["roiCiLoPct"] > 0:                 return "PASS" ...

Identical, including the reason strings, which are formatted character for
character the same way. EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED = 500 in both.

23.3 THE GRADED SLICE
`computeTierMetrics` drops voids and pushes before computing ROI.
`mlbgate.grade` does the same: `(~voided) & hit.notna()`.

23.4 THE BOOTSTRAP
2,000 iterations, percentile method, on the unit-profit series. Seed 20260701.
`exact=True` walks the harness's own mulberry32 PRNG stream and reproduces the
shipped CI bit for bit; `exact=False` draws the same percentile bootstrap from
numpy PCG64 and is used in the sweeps for speed.

23.6 FINAL MARKET STATUS (build_workbook.status_of, unchanged)

24. WALK-FORWARD VALIDATION

    warm-up            25% of rows (never scored)
    refit blocks       40
    boundary type      timestamp, not row index
    window             expanding
    minimum train fold 200 rows

25. FULL OOS EVALUATION
================================================================================

The FULL OUT-OF-SAMPLE period is every row outside the 25% warm-up window --
i.e. every row the walk-forward scored. For the final configuration that is 28,141 board rows
across 11 markets, 2024-04-01 to 2026-09-16.

    Gate (full OOS) PASS: 7 / 11
        batter_hits, totals, batter_total_bases, pitcher_strikeouts,
        batter_runs_scored, pitcher_outs, batter_strikeouts
    Full-OOS units: +559.3

WHAT THIS NUMBER IS AND IS NOT: the full OOS period is out of sample FOR THE
MODEL but its earlier half was used to CHOOSE the filter, the side policy and
the override. It is therefore an optimistic estimate. That is precisely why the
project reports a second gate on a window that was never read.

A WARNING ABOUT COMPARING CONFIGURATIONS: the full-OOS count is the count most
easily moved by things that have nothing to do with model quality. On a smaller,
earlier dataset it reads 9/11; on this one it reads 7/11 -- and both changes that
caused the fall make the test HARDER rather than the model worse. The verdict
windows move roughly three months later, and 2026 is 134,936 rows here rather
than 25,744 and is a materially harder period (section 29.4). ANY COMPARISON
BETWEEN TWO CONFIGURATIONS OF THIS SYSTEM MUST USE THE BOTH-GATES COUNT, NEVER
THE FULL-OOS COUNT ALONE.


================================================================================
26. VERDICT WINDOW EVALUATION
================================================================================

26.1 WHAT IT IS
The LATER HALF of each market's out-of-sample period, split at the median
commence time of the scored rows (`run_final.halves`). It is never read while
the global filter, the side policy or the per-market override is chosen. Every
one of those decisions is made on the SELECT half, and SELECT is itself split
into SELECT-A (choose) and SELECT-B (score) at three inner cuts.

26.2 THE RESULT
    Gate (verdict window) PASS: 6 / 11
        batter_hits, batter_total_bases, pitcher_strikeouts,
        batter_runs_scored, pitcher_outs, batter_strikeouts
    Verdict units: +202.7  over 12,687 picks

26.3 THE SECOND CONFIDENCE INTERVAL
Every verdict row carries TWO CI lower bounds:
  * `ciLo`       -- the gate's own pick-level bootstrap. This is what decides
                    PASS/FAIL, because it is what the shipped gate computes.
  * `clusCiLo`   -- a GAME-CLUSTERED bootstrap that resamples whole games rather
                    than picks. Picks on the same game settle together, so a
                    pick-level CI understates the spread whenever a board holds
                    several lines on one event.
The clustered bound is reported next to the gate's number everywhere and never
in place of it. On the six PASS markets the two agree closely (e.g. batter_hits
9.51 vs 9.24), which is evidence the pick-level CI is not badly understating.

27.4 WHAT THE VARIANTS TELL YOU
  * THE PLACEBO-LAG PROFILE IS FLAT TO SLIGHTLY RISING, NOT COLLAPSING. This is
    the single strongest piece of leakage evidence in the project. A same-game
    leak dies at the first day of lag. This does not. It rises modestly because
    lagged roll-ups are smoother and the boards are smaller, not because lag
    helps.
  * LINE SHOPPING IS WORTH REAL MONEY. Betting the median book instead of the
    best number at that line costs roughly two thirds of the verdict units
    (202.7 -> 73.4). Anyone assuming this edge survives worse execution should
    read that number first. THE HEADLINE RESULT ASSUMES BEST-PRICE EXECUTION.
  * The verdict count sits between 4 and 6 across every variant -- a broad
    stable region rather than a single lucky setting.

27.5 INDEPENDENCE AND SELECTION

was used during model selection: the global filter, the side policy and the
override are all chosen on the locked configuration's SELECT half, and the
variants are run afterwards as a stress test on the finished result.

28.3 WHY ev_pass VS ev_filtered
The distinction is the shipped one, not a choice made by the research. The
batter and pitcher write paths carry an `evPerUnit` and production shows a pick
only when it is positive (`isEvFilteredPick`). The game write path has no
`evPerUnit` at all, so production shows a game pick on confidence and the
heavy-juice veto alone (`isEvPassPick`). `PHASE1_SCOPE.md` s1b records this.
Measuring a game board through an EV filter it does not have in production would
not be measuring the product.

28.4 WHAT PARITY DOES AND DOES NOT MEAN
IT MEANS: if these 28,141 picks were handed to the production surface, none of
them would be filtered out as unservable.
IT DOES NOT MEAN: that these picks ARE what production shows, or that these
models are deployed. It is not. See section 1 and 30.4.

deployable artifact.

33.2 WHY NO LIVE PASS CAN BE CLAIMED -- THREE INDEPENDENT REASONS

REASON 1 -- THERE IS NO UNSETTLED BOARD.
The harness cannot produce one. `frames.load_candidates` keeps only rows whose
outcome exists; `features.graded` keeps only graded rows; `policy.walkforward`
needs `overHit` to fit anything. Every board row the validation pipeline can build
is, by construction, already settled. Pending count: 0, in every window.

REASON 2 -- THESE ROWS ARE NOT POST-DEPLOYMENT.
Every date in section 30 falls INSIDE the out-of-sample period that
produced the headline table, and for most markets inside the verdict window
itself. They are already counted in the 6/11. Reporting them again as a "live
result" would be double-counting the same picks.

REASON 3 -- THE SAMPLE IS FAR BELOW THE GATE'S FLOOR.
The largest per-market 30-day n is 462 (batter_runs_scored), below
EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED = 500. Every window therefore falls on the CI arm of the
gate, where 3 to 462 picks cannot produce a usable interval. The gate column in
`results/live_production_results.csv` is computed mechanically and is
explicitly flagged in that file's own header as NOT a market verdict.

33.3 THE SENTENCE THAT SHOULD BE USED
"No realized live-market PASS/FAIL result can be computed, because the final configuration
pipeline produces no unsettled board and the most recent available slates are
already inside the historical out-of-sample period."

37.1 THE THREE WARNINGS, STATED PLAINLY

WARNING 1 -- SELECTION-LAYER OPTIMISM. Every model prediction is out of sample,
but the global filter, the side policy and the per-market override were chosen
using the SELECT half of that same out-of-sample period. The full-OOS gate is
therefore optimistic. This is not a leak -- it is the reason the verdict window
exists, and the verdict window IS clean. The correct reading is: use the
BOTH-GATES count (6/11), never the full-OOS count alone.

WARNING 2 -- EVENT-BRIDGE CONTAMINATION. 0.87% of events resolved by the rebuilt
event_id <-> game_pk bridge are known-wrong (section 5.4). A wrong bridge grades
a candidate against a different game. It is measured, small, and carried.

WARNING 3 -- PARK FACTORS. Point-in-time correct but slow-moving, so a park's
season-to-date factor is highly correlated with its full-season factor. Not a
leak; a weak channel. Flagged, not repaired.

37.2 THE CONCLUSION, IN THE WORDING THE EVIDENCE SUPPORTS

NO CONFIRMED LEAKAGE WAS FOUND BY THE IMPLEMENTED AUDIT CHECKS.

That sentence is not the same as "there is no leakage", and this audit does not
make the stronger claim. The as-of sampling covers 3 feature families at 200
rows each, not all thirteen families exhaustively; the gate replay covers two
shipped reports; and the member cache was not rebuilt from raw data during this
audit. The strongest single piece of positive evidence is the placebo-lag
profile, because it is a test that WOULD have failed loudly if a same-game leak
existed, and it did not.

  3. CATBOOST IS UNTESTED. XGBoost and LightGBM are now installable but were not
     used in this work; HistGradientBoosting is the same histogram-boosting family
     LightGBM belongs to, so the genuinely untested ground is CatBoost's ordered
     target statistics.
  4. 2026 DEGRADATION IS REAL AND UNEXPLAINED (section 29.4). It may be market
     sharpening, or the 2026 rows may be priced differently in ways the T-6h fix
     does not fully capture (section 10.4). Worth isolating before trusting the
     board on live data.
  5. 313 DOUBLEHEADERS DROPPED in the expansion rather than guessed.
  6. `batter_total_bases` REMAINS FRAGILE: the unguarded side lever caused a 92%

     fragility is structural.
  7. THE FULL-OOS GATE COUNT IS NOT A STABLE YARDSTICK. It moves with the size
     and difficulty of the dataset, not only with model quality (section 25).
     Any comparison between two configurations of this system must use the
     BOTH-GATES count.

     optimisation here; it is load-bearing.
  9. NO CLV MEASUREMENT. The warehouse holds roughly 2 snapshots per event,
     which is too sparse to reconstruct line movement. CLV is the standard way
     to distinguish a real edge from a lucky one, and it is not available.
 10. THE VALIDATED MODELS ARE NOT DEPLOYED and there is no inference path for an
     unplayed game (section 30.4).

44. GENERALIZATION RISK
================================================================================

ASSESSMENT: MEDIUM.

EVIDENCE THAT LOWERS THE RISK
  * Six markets pass TWO gates, the second on a window never read during any
    selection decision.
  * Five of those six survive ALL SIX robustness variants; the sixth survives
    five.
  * The placebo-lag profile is flat to slightly rising -- the signature of a
    form signal, not a leak.
  * The verdict pass count sits between 4 and 6 across every variant: a broad
    stable region, not a single lucky setting.
  * Production parity is exact (0 of 28,141).
  * The whole result reproduces bit-identically.
  * The markets that fail, fail consistently across the gate, the robustness
    variants AND the independent E24 held-out test. The system's failures are
    coherent, which is itself evidence that its passes are not noise.

EVIDENCE THAT RAISES THE RISK
  * 2026 IS FIVE TIMES LESS PROFITABLE THAN 2025 on 9,476 picks. Whatever is
    happening, it is happening in the most recent and most relevant period.
  * The game markets are demonstrably being priced out. Nothing suggests this
    reverses.
  * The edge is overstated by 3-18 points at every predicted level (section
    19.3). The EV filter works as a threshold and not as a ranking, which means
    the system has less control over its own board than the numbers suggest.
  * Two thirds of the verdict units depend on best-price execution.
  * `batter_strikeouts`, one of the six PASS markets, is validated on 2024 only
    and is no longer offered.
  * `batter_total_bases`, another of the six, is structurally fragile.
  * No CLV, the standard forward-looking check, is available.
  * The selection layer saw the SELECT half of the same period. The verdict
    window mitigates this; it does not eliminate it.
  * There is no post-deployment evidence of any kind. Zero markets have been
    observed live.

THE HONEST SUMMARY
Six markets clear a two-gate bar that an extended programme of experiments failed to game,
and they survive a stress test designed to break them. That is a real result.
It is also a result measured entirely on history, in a period whose most recent
year is materially worse than its predecessor, with no live observation behind
it. A reasonable operator would ship a small number of markets in a monitored,
paper-traded mode first, watch CLV, and re-evaluate on the 2026 regime rather
than the pooled average.
