CIlo is the gate's own pick-level bootstrap lower bound -- it is what decides
PASS/FAIL. clusCIlo resamples whole GAMES rather than picks, because picks on
the same game settle together. It is reported beside the gate's number and never
in place of it. On the six PASS markets the two agree closely.


3. THE GATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    PASS if  graded n >= 500  AND  ROI > 0
    PASS if  graded n <  500  AND  95% ROI CI lower bound > 0
    FAIL otherwise

Implemented in harness/lib/metrics.ts (evaluateEvGate, EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED=500)
and ported line for line to harness/uplift/mlbgate.py. Verified by replaying
shipped harness reports through the port: 2 reports, 0 mismatches, CI reproduced
to full printed precision.

Final market status:
    both gates PASS                    -> PASS
    exactly one gate PASSes            -> PASS_WITH_RESTRICTIONS
    neither, and flat-bet ROI < -12%   -> VETO
    neither, otherwise                 -> FAIL_AFTER_ITERATION


4. THE TWO WINDOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FULL OOS      Every row outside the 25% walk-forward warm-up. Out of sample for
              the MODEL, but its earlier half was used to choose the filter, the
              side policy and the override. Optimistic by construction.

VERDICT       The LATER HALF of each market's out-of-sample period, split at the
              median commence time. NEVER READ while anything was chosen. Every
              selection decision is made on the SELECT half, itself split into
              SELECT-A (choose) and SELECT-B (score) at three inner cuts.

The BOTH-GATES count is the headline precisely because the verdict gate alone is
the clean number and the full-OOS gate alone is the optimistic one.

TWO THINGS THIS TABLE TELLS YOU
  * THE PLACEBO-LAG PROFILE IS FLAT TO SLIGHTLY RISING. A same-game leak dies at

    leakage evidence in the project.

    (202.7 -> 73.4). THE HEADLINE ASSUMES BEST-PRICE EXECUTION.

9. WHY EACH FAILING MARKET FAILED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    batter_home_runs   Product rule, not the model. The D-164 heavy-juice veto
                       plus confidence >= 60 leaves 29 board rows out of 59,338
                       graded candidates. Loosening a shipped rule to pass was
                       refused.
    h2h                Efficient market. No override qualifies; -6.16% on 639
                       verdict picks and -35.9 units in 2026.
    spreads            Efficient market. `price` beats the model by 0.0143 --
                       the largest gain measured anywhere -- and WAS adopted,
                       lifting verdict ROI from -7.45% to -2.91%. Still negative.
    totals             Underpowered by construction. 221 verdict picks, below
                       the gate's 500, so it needs the CI to clear zero -- about
                       13% flat ROI. No honest MLB board returns that.
    batter_rbis        Genuinely marginal. -0.66% on 1,020 verdict picks.
                       `compact` beats `gbm` but by 0.0005-0.0012, inside the
                       0.0011 noise band at two of three cuts.

Experiment E24 established independently, on held-out SELECT-B rather than on
the verdict window, that totals, spreads, h2h and batter_home_runs clear the
gate on 0 OF 3 INNER CUTS UNDER EVERY SELECTION RULE TESTED. The verdict table
is not bad luck.

NO REALIZED LIVE-MARKET PASS/FAIL RESULT CAN BE COMPUTED. Three independent
reasons:

  1. THE PIPELINE PRODUCES NO UNSETTLED BOARD. frames.load_candidates keeps only
     rows whose outcome exists and features.graded keeps only graded rows, so
     there is nothing pending to settle later. Pending count: 0, every window.
  2. THESE ROWS ARE NOT POST-DEPLOYMENT. Every date above falls inside the
     out-of-sample period and is already counted in Part 1. Quoting them
     again would be double-counting the same picks.
  3. THE SAMPLE IS BELOW THE GATE'S FLOOR. The largest per-market 30-day n is
     462, under EV_GATE_MIN_GRADED = 500, so every window falls on the CI arm
     where a few hundred picks cannot produce a usable interval.

The `gate` column in results/live_production_results.csv is computed
mechanically per window and is flagged in that file as NOT a market verdict. The
market verdicts are in results/final_matrix.csv and nowhere else.

PART 3 -- WHAT IS AND IS NOT DEPLOYED
================================================================================

THE VALIDATED MODELS ARE NOT IN PRODUCTION.

The live SharpAI product generates picks from
supabase/functions/_shared/scoring_mlb_v2.ts plus the algorithm_weights table,
written into recommendations_cache by scheduled edge functions. That is a
hand-weighted factor scorer.

The validated models (harness/uplift/*.py) are a separate codebase. They import
nothing from scoring_mlb_v2.ts and nothing imports them. This work is an offline
UPLIFT STUDY on the same priced universe, judged by the same gate.

Five things would be needed to serve a board live, and none exists today:
  1. an inference path that does not require a settled outcome;
  2. a persisted model -- zero estimators are saved today;
  3. a live odds feed at a lead matching the backtest's;
  4. feature parity at serve time;
  5. a decision about which markets ship.
