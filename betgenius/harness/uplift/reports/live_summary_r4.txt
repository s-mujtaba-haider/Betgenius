CAVEAT ON THE GATE COLUMN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The gate column applies the shipped rule (n>=500 -> ROI>0, else 95% CI lower
bound > 0) mechanically to each window. On a 1-, 7- or 30-day window no market
reaches n=500, so every evaluation falls on the CI arm, where a handful of picks
cannot produce a meaningful interval. These per-window gate values are NOT a
market verdict and must never be quoted as one. The market verdicts are in
results/final_matrix_r4v.csv and nowhere else.
